Task 2 · evalscope extension

Benchmark compression for a production prospect

Customer question: Is this model good enough for coding (LiveCodeBench v5) and long-context reasoning (AA-LCR)?

  • Full eval is too expensive for iterative model selection.
  • Goal: smallest sample that preserves ranking + go/no-go signal.
  • Part B (forward-looking): cheap MMMU encoder probe for multimodal roadmap.

Part A

Multi-objective pruning inside evalscope

  • Coverage — strata across metadata
  • Disagreement — samples where models diverge (ranking-sensitive)
  • Difficulty — avoid trivial/easy-only subsets
  • Facility-location greedy selection · not random / top-k hardest (forbidden baselines)
evalscope eval --datasets live_code_bench_pruned \
  --dataset-args '{"pruning_strategy":"multi_objective","prune_ratio":0.1}'
